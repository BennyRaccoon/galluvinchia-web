# Myths, Stories of the Gods

---


### The Gems of Origin
*A Myth of Panos*

Gems of immense power, each embodying a different force of existence, *Light, Growth, Time, Heat*, were scattered across Galluvinchia before memory began. One illuminates [An'Ramoda](../regions/cities/anramoda.md). The others have never been accounted for.

---

### The Cycle of Life
*A Myth of Brenadette*

When the Primordials fell, someone had to take watch over the Cycle of Life. Brenadette descended into the Neverender and has not left since. The perpetual light of Renewal blinded her. She has never slept again.

---

### Giants of the North
*A Myth of Aremedia*

A choleric madness spread among the giants of [An'Ramoda](../regions/cities/anramoda.md), threatening the city's peace. [Aremedia](../gods/aremedia.md) exiled them to the far reaches of the North. The giants have not forgotten.

---

### Moroes the Lover
*A Myth of Moroes and Morphia*

[Moroes](../gods/moroes.md) gifted the future gods tool after tool, each forged to the rhythm of his heartbeat, asking for Morphia's hand in return. When divinity was achieved, the wedding was cancelled. The heartbeat of his anvil, they say, is the only thing that stops the ache.

---

### The Beauty of Her Life
*A Myth of Morphia and Leeve*

When Morphia withdrew into sorrow, one of her devoted paladins swore an oath to restore her. Through patience and acts of kindness, she succeeded. Morphia, moved, elevated her to godhood — and she became **[Leeve](../gods/leeve.md)**, goddess of beauty and nature.
